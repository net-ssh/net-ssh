Added `Net::SSH.default_configuration`, a callable receiving the host and its ssh_config options and returning default options for every `Net::SSH.start`, including connections opened by net-sftp, net-scp or SSHKit. Its options override ssh_config and are overridden by options passed to `start`; an option passed as `nil` counts as not given. It can't set `:user` or `:config`, and the options it changes are validated like those passed to `start`. Thanks @fatkodima [#986]
